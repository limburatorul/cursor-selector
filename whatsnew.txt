# What's new, built into the exe (build.cmd: /resource whatsnew.txt). Newest first.
# "## version | yyyy-mm-dd | title", then one "- " line per point.

## 1.5.0 | 2026-11-23 | A Windows 11 look, and cursors you can try
- Cursor Selector now looks like the rest of Windows 11: the same greys, your own accent colour, Segoe UI and plain headings, without the glow it had before.
- Every scheme in the list shows three of its cursors (normal, link and busy), so Bibata Amber and Bibata Ice no longer look alike.
- Point at a cursor and your pointer turns into it, so you can try a scheme before you apply it.
- Apply sits next to the scheme's name. Double-click a scheme or press Enter to apply it.
- Import is the + beside the search. Open library folder, Refresh list and Check for updates are under the ... button.
- The bar at the bottom names the scheme Windows uses. Click the name to jump to it in the list.
- The app is less than half its old size: it uses the fonts that come with Windows.

## 1.4.3 | 2026-11-03 | The pizza button, easier to find
- Buy me a pizza is red now, so it stands out in the sidebar, and it glows twice every 10 seconds. It stays still while the window is minimized, while your pointer is on it, or when Windows' animation effects are off.
- It still only opens our Buy Me a Coffee page in your browser, and it is still optional.

## 1.4.2 | 2026-11-01 | Buy me a pizza
- Buy me a pizza, a new button in the sidebar. It is optional and opens our Buy Me a Coffee page in your browser. It goes toward code signing and a Steam listing.

## 1.4.1 | 2026-10-25 | A new icon
- A new icon, brighter and easier to spot in the taskbar. It is on the window, the sidebar and the Start menu.
- What's new now shows a picture for each release.

## 1.4.0 | 2026-10-23 | One home for your cursors, and this window
- What's new lives here now: every release, by month. It opens once after an update, and any time from What's new in the sidebar.
- Your cursor library and the backup of your original cursors moved to %APPDATA%\ProtagonistLabs\Cursor Selector, beside the other Protagonist Labs apps. It happened by itself the first time 1.4 started.
- The scheme you are using keeps working: Windows is pointed at the cursors' new place.
- Cursor Selector is still one portable .exe. Open Library folder takes you to the new place.

## 1.3.2 | 2026-10-17 | An exe that says whose it is
- The .exe names Protagonist Labs and its version, so fewer antivirus tools mistake it for something else.

## 1.3.1 | 2026-10-16 | Hourly update checks
- Checks for a new version every hour, and says so when a check could not reach GitHub.

## 1.3.0 | 2026-10-15 | Cursor Selector updates itself
- A newer version is downloaded in the background, checked, and used the next time you open the app.
- Click the name and version at the top of the sidebar to check yourself.

## 1.2.0 | 2026-10-15 | The shared app card
- Cursor Selector lets the other Protagonist Labs apps know it is installed. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- If you own one of our paid apps, a quiet line lists the others at 30% off.

## 1.1.0 | 2026-10-10 | A face of its own
- The brand's typefaces travel inside the app, each cursor sits on a chip its own size, and the 17 roles are grouped by the job they do.
- Every scheme shows its arrow in the list, with search (Ctrl+F) and a closer look at any single cursor.

## 1.0.2 | 2026-09-26 | Links that say where they came from
- Website and More apps tell protagonistlabs.app the visit came from Cursor Selector.

## 1.0.1 | 2026-09-26 | Feedback, and packs in the right order
- Send feedback and Website under the library buttons.

## 1.0.0 | 2026-09-18 | The first Cursor Selector
- Previews all 17 cursor roles of a scheme, applies it in one click, and imports a folder or a .zip straight into the library.
