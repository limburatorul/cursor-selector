## 1.4.0 | 2026-10-23 | One home for your cursors, and this window
- What's new lives here now: every release, by month. It opens once after an update, and any time from What's new in the sidebar.
- Your cursor library and the backup of your original cursors moved to %APPDATA%\ProtagonistLabs\Cursor Selector, beside the other Protagonist Labs apps. It happened by itself the first time 1.4 started.
- The scheme you are using keeps working: Windows is pointed at the cursors' new place.
- Cursor Selector is still one portable .exe. Open Library folder takes you to the new place.

## 1.3.2 | 2026-10-17 | An exe that says whose it is
- The .exe names Protagonist Labs and its version, so fewer antivirus tools mistake it for something else.

## 1.3.1 | 2026-10-16 | Hourly update checks
- Checks for a new version every hour, and says so when a check could not reach GitHub.

## 1.3.0 | 2026-10-15 | Cursor Selector updates itself
- A newer version is downloaded in the background, checked, and used the next time you open the app.
- Click the name and version at the top of the sidebar to check yourself.

## 1.2.0 | 2026-10-15 | The shared app card
- Cursor Selector lets the other Protagonist Labs apps know it is installed. Nothing is sent anywhere.

- Every scheme shows its arrow in the list, with search (Ctrl+F) and a closer look at any single cursor.
